Level 1

Level 1 final test

Thirty questions across all six modules.

Week 4 · Day 2830 questionsAbout 45 min✓ Done

This test covers all six modules — five questions from each. Take it in one sitting, without looking back at the pages. 24 or more out of 30 means you are ready for Level 2; below that, revisit the modules where you lost marks (the explanation under each answer names the page).

Final test · question 1 of 30

Which international standard describes the field / automation / management levels of building automation?

ISO 16484 covers building automation and control systems. (See page 1.2.)
Final test · question 2 of 30

The target value a controller tries to reach is the…

The setpoint is the target; the process variable is the measured value; the error is the difference. (See page 1.1.)
Final test · question 3 of 30

After a long power cut a controller runs, but its AHU now starts at the wrong time each day. Most likely cause?

Correct schedule, wrong time — check the real-time clock battery and set up NTP time sync. (See page 1.3.)
Final test · question 4 of 30

An alarm returns to normal before anyone acknowledges it. What happens?

Unacknowledged events stay visible so short faults are not missed. (See page 1.4.)
Final test · question 5 of 30

In a well-segregated DDC panel, which terminals sit on the 24 V signal side?

Sensor, AO, DI, DO and RS485 terminals form the ELV group, kept apart from 230 V. (See page 1.5.)
Final test · question 6 of 30

What is a TFA?

Treated fresh air unit. (See page 2.1.)
Final test · question 7 of 30

Fresh fraction from MAT 26 °C, RAT 24 °C, OAT 34 °C is…

(26 − 24) ÷ (34 − 24) = 0.2. (See page 2.2.)
Final test · question 8 of 30

A pressure-independent VAV box controls…

Its flow loop holds the airflow set by the zone temperature loop. (See page 2.4.)
Final test · question 9 of 30

Cooling delivered by 10 L/s of chilled water with a 5.5 K temperature difference is about…

4.19 × 10 × 5.5 ≈ 230 kW, about 66 TR. (See page 2.5.)
Final test · question 10 of 30

Which mode overrides every other operating mode?

Fire mode overrides everything. (See page 2.7.)
Final test · question 11 of 30

Why is PT1000 preferred to PT100 for 2-wire BMS inputs?

3.85 Ω/°C vs 0.385 Ω/°C. (See page 3.1.)
Final test · question 12 of 30

What does DCV adjust?

Demand-controlled ventilation varies fresh air with occupancy. (See page 3.2.)
Final test · question 13 of 30

Kv for 6 m³/h at 0.36 bar valve drop is…

6 ÷ √0.36 = 6 ÷ 0.6 = 10. (See page 3.6.)
Final test · question 14 of 30

A VFD ignores the BMS start command but runs from its keypad. Check first…

The drive must be in Auto with the control source set to terminals or fieldbus. (See page 3.7.)
Final test · question 15 of 30

A fresh air damper actuator is usually…

Closed on power loss keeps unconditioned air out. (See page 3.5.)
Final test · question 16 of 30

A DI terminal measures 230 V AC. What is this?

DI wetting voltages are low; 230 V means a foreign voltage. (See page 4.1.)
Final test · question 17 of 30

Which BACnet object type is a setpoint stored only in the program?

Software points are Value objects. (See page 4.2.)
Final test · question 18 of 30

A transmitter needs 12 V on a 24 V loop. Maximum loop resistance?

(24 − 12) ÷ 0.02 = 600 Ω. (See page 4.3.)
Final test · question 19 of 30

A triac output can switch…

Triacs are AC-only switches. (See page 4.4.)
Final test · question 20 of 30

Earthing a cable screen at both ends can cause…

Current flows in the screen between earth points. (See page 4.5.)
Final test · question 21 of 30

Where are RS485 terminators fitted?

120 Ω at each physical end. (See page 5.1.)
Final test · question 22 of 30

BACnet/IP's default UDP port is…

47808 = 0xBAC0. (See page 5.2.)
Final test · question 23 of 30

Modbus exception 02 means…

The register does not exist. (See page 5.3.)
Final test · question 24 of 30

To remove an operator override at BACnet priority 8 you…

Writing a new value leaves the override in place. (See page 5.4.)
Final test · question 25 of 30

All 20 MS/TP devices went offline just after one new device was added. First action?

One mismatched device can upset the whole segment. (See page 5.5.)
Final test · question 26 of 30

Why wait 60 s after the fan is proven before enabling the PID loop?

Loops enabled during start-up transients overreact. (See page 6.1.)
Final test · question 27 of 30

Safety interlocks are…

They must work if the BMS fails and in every mode. (See page 6.2.)
Final test · question 28 of 30

Most HVAC loops use…

Derivative amplifies noise; HVAC is slow. (See page 6.3.)
Final test · question 29 of 30

The filter-dirty alarm should be enabled…

ΔP is meaningless when the fan is off or starting. (See page 6.5.)
Final test · question 30 of 30

Optimum start calculates…

Using the temperature difference and learned pull-down rate. (See page 6.6.)
