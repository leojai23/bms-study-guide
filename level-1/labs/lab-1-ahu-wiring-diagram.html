Practical lab 1

AHU control wiring diagram

Draw AHU-01's control wiring: power, every point from controller terminal to field device, and the terminal schedule.

Week 4 · Day 28About 2 hours✓ Done

Time

About 2 hours

You will produce

A wiring diagram for panel DDC-AHU-01 and a terminal schedule.

Read first

1.5 BMS control panel architecture
4.3 Analog signals: 0–10 V, 4–20 mA, 0–20 mA
4.4 Dry contacts, relay outputs, NO and NC
4.5 Sensor wiring and troubleshooting
3.5 Damper actuators
3.7 Variable frequency drives (VFD)

What you need

  • Pen and paper, or a free drawing tool such as draw.io
  • Your Lab 2 point list (terminals and cable numbers)
  • Datasheet figures for VA and current (the example figures below are fine)

Tasks

1 · Power section

Draw the 230 V supply through the MCB (Q1) to the control transformer (T1, 230/24 V AC) and the 24 V DC supply (G1), each with its own fuse. Size T1: controller 20 VA + valve actuator 8 VA + damper actuator 6 VA + relay coil 1 VA ≈ 35 VA — choose a standard size with plenty of margin. Size G1 for the 4–20 mA loop and the two transmitters.

2 · Inputs

Draw each input from its UI terminal to the X1 terminal strip, its cable number, and the field device. Show the NTC sensors as 2-wire, the RH and CO₂ transmitters as 3-wire (24 V, 0 V, signal), the pressure transmitter as a 2-wire loop, and the DI contacts as volt-free pairs.

3 · Outputs

DO1 to the coil of interposing relay KA1; KA1's NO contact to the VFD run input. AO1 and AO3 with the 24 V AC supply to their actuators (3 cores each). AO2 to the VFD's speed input.

4 · Communication and fire

Add RS485 (A, B, common) to the VFD with terminators at both ends. Show the FAS contact to UI10 and note that a second FAS contact is wired into the starter circuit upstream of the HOA (page 6.2).

5 · Terminal schedule

List every terminal: number, wire/core, cable, destination, signal. Mark spares.

6 · Check

Trace every point in your Lab 2 list through the drawing. Then check segregation: 230 V terminals apart from 24 V signals.

Power sizing

T1: 35 VA load → choose 100 VA (margin for actuators moving together and future additions). G1: loop 20 mA + two transmitters ~ 2 × 30 mA ≈ 0.1 A → a 1 A 24 V DC supply is ample. Fuses: F1 2 A on 24 V AC, F2 1 A on 24 V DC; Q1 6 A MCB on the 230 V supply.
