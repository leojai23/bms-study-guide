AHU-01 — sequence of operation

1 General. AHU-01 serves the Level 3 offices. It is a constant-volume recirculating AHU with a CHW coil (2-way valve), a VFD-driven supply fan, a modulating spring-return-closed fresh air damper and a manual return damper. It is controlled by DDC-AHU-01. Point names refer to the AHU-01 point list.

2 Modes. Off · Starting · Running · Stopping · Fire shutdown · Fan-fail lockout · Hand (local).

3 Enable. AHU-01 shall run when OCC is Occupied or Override AND SF-AM = Auto AND FIRE = Normal AND no fan-fail lockout AND the minimum off time (3 min, adj.) has elapsed.

4 Start.

  1. Command FAD to FAD-MIN (20 %, adj.).
  2. Command SF-CMD On and SF-SPD to the speed setpoint (84 % = 42 Hz, adj.).
  3. If SF-STS is not Running within 30 s (adj.): raise “AHU-01 supply fan failed to start” (high), command SF-CMD Off, FAD 0 %, enter fan-fail lockout.
  4. After SF-STS Running for 60 s (adj.), enable the RAT and SAT low-limit loops and CO₂ control.
  5. Arm the temperature and humidity alarms 15 min (adj.) after the fan is proven.

5 Normal control. CHWV = min(PI on RAT to RAT-SP 24 °C, PI on SAT to SAT-LL 11 °C), 0–100 %. FAD = max(FAD-MIN, DCV), where DCV ramps from FAD-MIN at 700 ppm to 50 % at 1,000 ppm RACO2 (adj.). SF-SPD fixed.

6 Stop. On OCC Unoccupied: disable loops and drive CHWV to 0 %; command SF-CMD Off; if SF-STS remains Running 60 s after, raise “failed to stop” (high); command FAD 0 %; start the minimum off timer.

7 Safeties and interlocks. The FAS contact, the high static pressure cut-out (manual reset) and the VFD healthy contact are hard-wired in series upstream of the HOA switch. On FIRE: the fan stops by the hard-wired trip; the BMS sets SF-CMD Off, CHWV and FAD 0 %, and raises a critical alarm; restart requires FAS reset and operator reset (SF-RST). CHWV and FAD are 0 % whenever SF-STS is not Running.

8 Alarms. As in the point list (page 6.5 settings).

9 Failure responses. Fan trips while running (SF-STS lost > 10 s): as failure to start. SF-AM = Manual while the schedule requires running: “not in Auto” (medium). RAT sensor fault: alarm; CHWV fixed at 50 % (adj.). SAT sensor fault: alarm; low-limit loop disabled. RACO2 fault: FAD to 35 % (adj.). Modbus loss 60 s: alarm (low); hard-wired control continues. Power return: wait 60 s (AHU-01 stagger, adj.), then re-evaluate enable.
