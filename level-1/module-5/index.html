Level 1 · Module 5

BMS Communication Protocols

RS485, addressing, Modbus RTU/TCP, BACnet MS/TP and BACnet/IP, and fixing comms faults.

Communication is what turns separate controllers and devices into one system. This module starts with the RS485 cable and addressing, then covers the two protocols you will use most — Modbus and BACnet — and finishes with a method for finding communication faults. The subnet checker (5.2) and Modbus decoder (5.3) are there to practise with; Lab 5 puts Modbus into your hands with free tools.
