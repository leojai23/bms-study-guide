Warm-up · 3 quick questions from earlier pages (2 min)
  1. Where should an outdoor air temperature sensor be mounted?

    Show answer
    In the shade (north-facing), in a ventilated radiation shield, away from exhausts, condensing units and hot roof surfaces.
  2. List the sections of a typical AHU in airflow order.
    from 2.1 Air handling unit (AHU)
    Show answer
    Dampers and mixing box → pre-filter → fine filter → cooling coil (with drain pan) → heating coil if fitted → supply fan → discharge/silencer.
  3. Which AHU-01 VFD signals are hard-wired, and why?
    from 1.6 HVAC system integration
    Show answer
    Start/stop, run status, fault and speed reference — so the fan can be controlled and monitored even if the Modbus link fails.
In one line

An FCU is a small, local air-conditioning unit — a fan blowing room air over a chilled water coil — serving one room or zone, usually controlled by its own room thermostat or small controller.

?Why it matters

A large building can have hundreds of FCUs — in hotel rooms, cabins, meeting rooms. Individually they are simple, but together they are a big share of the BMS points, the network traffic and the comfort complaints. Knowing how an FCU and its thermostat work lets you fix most room-level problems quickly.

Types and piping

Ceiling concealed (ducted)

Hidden above the ceiling with short ducts to diffusers. Offices, hotels.

Cassette

Ceiling-mounted with a 4-way outlet. Shops, meeting rooms.

Wall or floor mounted

Exposed units; older buildings and retrofits.

2-pipe vs 4-pipe

2-pipe: one coil, chilled water only (or changeover). 4-pipe: separate cooling and heating coils, each with a valve.

How the thermostat controls it

  1. The thermostat measures room temperature and compares it with the setpoint (e.g. 24 °C).
  2. If the room is warmer than the setpoint plus half the deadband (a small band, e.g. ±0.5 K, in which nothing changes), it opens the valve.
  3. The further the room is above setpoint, the higher the fan speed: low, then medium, then high (or a higher 0–10 V signal on an EC fan).
  4. When the room is satisfied the valve closes. The fan either keeps running at low speed (for air movement and fresh air) or stops, depending on the setting.
Why a deadband?

Without one, an on/off valve would open and close every few seconds as the temperature crossed the setpoint, wearing out the actuator and annoying occupants. The deadband gives the room some room to drift before the controller acts.

Fresh air for FCU zones

An FCU only recirculates room air. Fresh air usually comes from a separate TFA unit through ducts into each room or into the FCU's return plenum. If the TFA stops, the FCUs still cool — but the rooms go stuffy.

Follow AHU-01

The meeting rooms and cabins on AHU-01's floor use FCUs with networked thermostats on a BACnet MS/TP field bus to the floor's supervisory controller (the “FCU controller” box in the page 1.2 diagram). Their schedules follow AHU-01's, so the whole floor switches together.

⚒On site

  • Check where the thermostat is mounted: not in sun, not near a door, not above a printer or kettle.
  • Many valve actuators have a manual-open lever or button for flushing — find out if one was left open.
  • Lift a ceiling tile and look at the drain pan when investigating a leak or smell.
  • On a new network of FCUs, check every address is unique before connecting the bus (Module 5).
  • Compare the thermostat reading with a hand-held thermometer at the same spot.

AKey terms

FCU
Fan coil unit: fan plus chilled water coil serving one room or zone.
2-pipe / 4-pipe
One coil with one supply and return, or separate cooling and heating coils.
Deadband
A band around the setpoint in which the controller takes no action.
EC motor
Electronically commutated fan motor with stepless speed control, usually 0–10 V.
Thermal actuator
Slow on/off valve actuator driven by a small heater element.
Occupancy mode
Occupied, unoccupied or standby — changes setpoints and fan behaviour.
Condensate pump
Small pump lifting condensate to a drain when gravity drainage is not possible.

✓Check yourself

Try each one before opening the answer.

Fault scenario · what would you do?

A meeting room is too cold. Its thermostat shows setpoint 24 °C, room 20 °C, fan on low, valve status closed. The CHW pipe after the valve is cold to the touch. What is the likely cause?

The controller has closed the valve, yet chilled water still flows — the pipe after the valve is cold. The valve is passing: stuck, damaged, or left on manual override. Check the actuator and the manual lever.

  1. What does an FCU not provide that an AHU does?

    Show answer
    Fresh air — an FCU only recirculates room air. Fresh air comes from a separate TFA.

  2. Why does an FCU thermostat use a deadband?

    Show answer
    To stop the valve and fan switching on and off rapidly around the setpoint.

  3. Explain the difference between 2-pipe and 4-pipe FCUs.

    Show answer
    2-pipe has one coil with one supply and return pipe (cooling only, or changeover). 4-pipe has separate cooling and heating coils, each with its own pipes and valve.

  4. Name four points a BMS typically reads or writes on a networked FCU.

    Show answer
    Any four of: on/off, occupancy mode, setpoint, room temperature, fan speed, valve status, alarms.

  5. A whole floor of FCUs goes offline at once. What kind of fault does that suggest?

    Show answer
    A shared fault: the field-bus wiring, termination, power, or the supervisory controller — not the individual units.
