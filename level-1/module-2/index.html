Level 1 · Module 2

HVAC Control Fundamentals

The plant a BMS controls: AHUs, FCUs, VAV/CAV, chilled water, chillers and cooling towers.

Before you can control plant, you need to know how it works. This module covers the equipment a BMS spends most of its time on — AHUs, FCUs, VAV systems, chillers and cooling towers — and ends with the language used to describe how they should run: the sequence of operation.

Module 2 quiz · question 1 of 10

In airflow order, which comes first in a typical AHU?

Air passes dampers and mixing box, then filters, then the coil, then the fan. (See page 2.1.)
Module 2 quiz · question 2 of 10

How does a chilled water coil remove moisture from air?

Condensed water collects in the drain pan and is drained away. (See page 2.1.)
Module 2 quiz · question 3 of 10

20 % fresh air at 35 °C mixes with 80 % return air at 24 °C. What is the mixed air temperature?

0.2 × 35 + 0.8 × 24 = 26.2 °C. (See page 2.2.)
Module 2 quiz · question 4 of 10

A building should normally be kept…

Slightly positive keeps dust and humid air out; toilets and kitchens are kept negative. (See page 2.2.)
Module 2 quiz · question 5 of 10

Where does an FCU zone usually get its fresh air?

FCUs recirculate room air; fresh air comes from a treated fresh air unit. (See page 2.3.)
Module 2 quiz · question 6 of 10

In a VAV system, what does the AHU's VFD usually control?

As boxes close, duct pressure rises and the fan slows to hold the pressure setpoint. (See page 2.4.)
Module 2 quiz · question 7 of 10

A fan slows to 80 % speed. Roughly how much power does it now need?

Power ∝ speed³: 0.8³ ≈ 0.51. (See page 2.4.)
Module 2 quiz · question 8 of 10

What must be proven before a chiller is enabled?

A chiller must never run without proven water flow. (See page 2.5.)
Module 2 quiz · question 9 of 10

Condenser water leaving a cooling tower can never be colder than…

Evaporative cooling is limited by the wet-bulb temperature. (See page 2.6.)
Module 2 quiz · question 10 of 10

Which sequence statement is testable?

It has a condition, a time limit, a proof and an action. (See page 2.7.)
