Level 1 · Module 6

BMS Control Logic

Start/stop sequences, interlocks, PID, valve and damper control, alarms and schedules.

This module brings everything together: the AHU-01 start/stop sequence, the interlocks and safeties around it, PID loops and how to tune them, the patterns used to control valves and dampers, alarms worth receiving, and schedules. Two interactive tools help — the AHU-01 sequence stepper (6.1) and the PID playground (6.3).
