BMS Architecture

What a BMS is, how it is layered, and the controllers, servers and panels that make it work.

This module gives you the big picture before the detail. By the end you will be able to sketch a BMS from the sensors up to the operator's screen, explain what a DDC controller does on every scan, and describe what is inside a control panel.

Module 1 quiz

Module 1 quiz · question 1 of 10

Which of the five jobs of a BMS keeps a record of values over time?

Trend logs record values over time so you can see when and why something changed. (See page 1.1.)
Module 1 quiz · question 2 of 10

Which building system does a BMS normally only monitor, never control?

Life-safety systems stay independent; the BMS receives their signals for monitoring only. (See page 1.1.)
Module 1 quiz · question 3 of 10

A supervisory (network) controller sits at which level?

It is an automation-level device that manages field controllers and routes between networks. (See page 1.2.)
Module 1 quiz · question 4 of 10

The BMS server is shut down for an upgrade. What happens to AHU-01?

Control lives in the controller. Only visibility, central alarms and long-term trending are lost. (See page 1.2.)
Module 1 quiz · question 5 of 10

Put the scan cycle in order:

Read and convert the inputs, run the logic, write the outputs, then exchange data — and repeat. (See page 1.3.)
Module 1 quiz · question 6 of 10

A universal input can be configured as…

Universal inputs accept resistance, voltage, current or contact signals, chosen in software. (See page 1.3.)
Module 1 quiz · question 7 of 10

Why is an interposing relay fitted between a controller DO and an MCC starter?

The controller switches only the relay's 24 V coil; the relay's own contact switches the field circuit. (See page 1.5.)
Module 1 quiz · question 8 of 10

A fan's HOA switch is in Hand. What does that mean for the BMS?

Only in Auto does the BMS have control. That is why auto/manual status is wired back as a DI. (See page 1.5.)
Module 1 quiz · question 9 of 10

Following “hard-wire what must work”, which VFD signal is hard-wired rather than read over Modbus?

Start/stop, run status, fault and speed reference are hard-wired so the fan works and is monitored if comms fail. (See page 1.6.)
Module 1 quiz · question 10 of 10

A chiller's CHW supply temperature shows 70 °C on the BMS and 7.0 °C on the chiller. What is wrong?

Exactly ten times too large means communication works but the register's scale was not applied. (See page 1.6.)
